# Changelog

Changes in each Hushfacebook version, newest first. Versions 0.1.0 to 0.19.0 are in the [changelog archive](docs/changelog-archive.md).

## 0.25.0 (2026-11-25)

* Hushfacebook v0.25.0 has 128 patches for Facebook 588.0.0.42.55. It needs Morphe Manager 2.7.0 or newer, or morphe-desktop 2.3.0 on a computer. Facebook stays on 588.0.0.42.55, so patch the same build with the same signing key and your settings carry over. Chats inside Facebook can take a background color of your own, `Hide posts from people, Pages and sites` takes groups now, and long videos in the Reels viewer get their side buttons back. The Hush settings follow your phone's light or dark theme and come in Arabic, Bulgarian, Italian and Korean too. `Unlock app icons` is gone.

### Added

* `Fill every reel` sits under `Fill the screen with reels`. With both on, every reel fills the screen even when that cuts a lot off the sides, so an unfolded foldable or a tablet loses the bars without a pinch on each reel. (#571)
* Chats that open inside Facebook can take a background color of your own. Type a color code in `Chat background` under Chats and Messenger and it goes behind the messages in every chat. `Last chat's background` gives the chat you opened last a color of its own. Two codes, like `#0D47A1 #FF6F00`, fade from top to bottom. It covers any theme the chat has, only your phone shows it, and nobody in the chat is told. (#573)
* The Hush settings now come in Bulgarian too. They follow your phone's language, like the other translations. (#572)
* The Hush settings now come in Arabic, translated by hamedhani1998. (#549)
* `Hide posts from people, Pages and sites` takes groups now. Put a group's name or ID on the list, or a link to the group, and posts in that group leave Home, along with friends' shares of them. Handy for a group you're still in but don't want in your feed, since Facebook keeps showing those after you unfollow. A link only works when it has the group's number in it, like facebook.com/groups/1234567890. (#550)
* `Show saved posts in the row` under `Play saved videos in a row` stops on a saved post instead of skipping it, so you can read it. Swipe up once you're past its end and the next saved video opens, or swipe down at its top to go back to the one before. While there's more of the post to scroll, your swipe just scrolls it. It's off until you turn it on. (#404)
* `Mark all notifications as read` sits at the top of Notifications in the Hush settings. One tap clears every unread dot in the Notifications tab. Facebook only puts its own Mark all as read in the three-dot menu there, and some accounts never get that menu. (Discussion #338)
* `Side buttons on long videos` under Reels and Watch puts Like, Comment, Share and Save back down the side of long videos in the Reels viewer, like a live replay or a film you open from Saved or the Reels library. Facebook only gives those the three dots. It starts off. (#525)
* The Hush settings now go light or dark with your phone's theme, like HushGram's do. Without the `Material You theme` patch they used to stay dark on every phone. `Force dark mode` still keeps them dark. (#559)
* The Hush settings now come in Italian and Korean too. They follow your phone's language, like the other translations. (#558)
* `Show Comment on posts with comments off` under News feed keeps the Comment button on posts whose author turned comments off or limited who can comment, so you can still read the comments already there. It's off until you turn it on. (#299)
* `Tap the video to bring them back` sits under Burn-in guard. Once the buttons on a reel fade, a tap on the video brings them back without pausing it. The next tap pauses like always, and holding for 2x still works. It's off until you turn it on. (#523)
* `Faint Clean mode button` under Reels and Watch makes Facebook's button for leaving Clean mode see-through, so the text in the video under it stays readable. It still works when you tap it, and it starts off. (#519)
* `Material You theme` has a switch now, under Colors and theme in the Hush settings. Turn it off, restart Facebook, and it's back to Facebook's own colors without patching again. Turn it back on the same way. Your accent color and AMOLED black still work with it off. The switch shows on Android 11 and up. (Discussion #527)
* `Hide Save under posts` sits with the other hide switches under News feed. Facebook has started putting its own Save button under posts for some accounts, and this takes it out. If `Save button under posts` is on too, hiding wins. (#541)
* `Show the time on every story` under Stories puts how long ago a story was posted next to the name on stories that leave it out, like a Page's. Friends' stories already show it, and they stay as they are. It's off until you turn it on. (#563)
* With Debug logging on, a Debug report now says what Burn-in guard kept bright and what it dimmed on the last story it dimmed, view by view with each one's size. A story that comes out wrong with the guard on, like a note story, can be pinned down from the report. (#523)
* `Hide reaction counts on comments` under Comments takes the number off the reaction faces on each comment. The faces stay, and tapping them still shows who reacted. Facebook's own setting for hiding reaction counts leaves comments alone. It's off until you turn it on. (#483)
* `Hide Stars and gifts under posts` sits with the other hide switches under News feed. It takes out the button some creators' posts get for sending them Stars or gifts. It starts off. (#483)
* `Show Videos in search` under Search puts a Videos chip in the row over search results, next to Posts, so a search can show just videos. It starts off. (#483)
* `Share to Facebook chats` under Chats and Messenger puts `Facebook chats` in Android's share menu, for when your chats are inside Facebook. A photo or video you share there goes to your gallery, and text or a link goes to your clipboard. Then Facebook opens on your chats, so you can pick one and attach it or paste it there. Facebook carries Messenger's own share screens but none of the code behind them, so the share menu had no way into a chat before. It starts off. (#568)
* `Post date after the caption` under Reels and Watch moves a reel's date to the end of its caption, for when the song, "AI content" and other labels crowd the line under the name. It works with `Show the post date on reels` on, and it starts off. (#490)

### Changed

* `Unlock app icons` is gone, along with its `Use the icon at the top of Home` switch and `Pick an app icon` row. Your other patch choices in Morphe Manager stay as they were. This selection reset only covers the removed patch, so no manual re-selection is needed.

### Fixed

* Leaving Facebook while a video you opened full screen from the feed plays puts just the video in the picture-in-picture window again. Facebook kept the window tied to the feed's player, which no longer had the video, so the whole screen shrank into the window with the comment box under it, and it stayed small in a corner after you opened it back up. The same happened with a video opened from a Page's reels. (#495)
* `Dark box behind reel names` now covers the date and details under a reel's name too ("AI content", the song), all in one box. Before, the box sat behind the name alone and its bottom edge ran through the date, which looked like a dark line under the name. `Dark box behind feed video names` does the same for the date under a name on a feed video. (#490)
* A reel with no caption no longer gets a thin dark line under its name when `Dark box behind folded captions` or `Dark box behind open captions` is on. Facebook still makes an empty caption there, and the box went behind it with nothing to cover. (#490)
* `Dark box behind reel names` covers group reels now, with the line under the group's name (who posted it, or the song) in the same box. Group reels draw that part their own way, so it never got a box before. (#490)
* In a Page's reels, the "1 day ago" line under an open caption gets the dark box too when `Dark box behind open captions` or `Dark box behind folded captions` is on. Before, the caption had its box and the date right under it didn't. (#490)
* Facebook no longer closes the moment it opens when `Disable Play Store updates` is picked twice, once in Hushfacebook and once in Morphe's own patches. Morphe's newer test builds of that patch and ours kept handing the version check back and forth until Facebook crashed. Either one alone was always fine, and now both together are too. (#561)
* `Load whole reply threads` loads long reply threads all the way again. On Facebook 588 a thread stopped after its first page or so, and the View more replies button was gone too, so there was no way to read the rest. Now each page comes in by itself as you scroll down to it. (#536)
* Switching `Tab bar while scrolling` between hiding and staying on screen no longer leaves short reels on the next start. With see-through bars or the rounded tab bar, the first reels after the switch stopped short and the next one peeked in under the tab bar until Reels was opened again. (#552)
* With `Hide the video comment box` on, the seek bar on a video you tap in Home sits where Facebook puts it again, above the Up next bar. It had dropped to the bottom edge, under Up next and into the swipe area, which made it hard to grab. A reel from the Reels row in Home now keeps its seek bar above Up next too. Turn on `Hide Up next` as well and the reel gets that room instead, with the seek bar down near the bottom. (#552)
* A reel you open from the Reels row in Home no longer prints its time over the caption when `Hide the video comment box`, `Hide Up next on videos` and `Keep the progress bar` are all on. The video now stops a little short of the bottom edge, which leaves the caption room above the time, and the seek bar stays well clear of the swipe area. (#552)
* With `See-through bars` on, the tab bar is frosted again while the feed moves under it. Since 0.24.0 the blur only flashed for a moment and then left the bar plainly see-through, most of all over a playing video. (#553)
* Facebook stops draining the battery in the background when you leave it with a paused video that was still loading. Facebook's player checks on a loading video a hundred times a second, playing or not, and stops loading once you leave, so a preview it hadn't finished (most often the Reels row on Home) kept waking the phone until Android closed Facebook. Now that check runs once a second while no Facebook screen is open. A video playing in the background with `Keep playing in the background` plays on as before. (#548)
* With `Always use Clean mode` on, a reel you take out of Clean mode no longer gets a second Clean mode button stacked on More options and the button above it, where it took their taps. The way back in is the Clean mode button down the side of the reel now, which shows whenever `Always use Clean mode` is on, even with `Clean mode button on reels` off. (#554)
* `Fold long posts` now reaches the posts Facebook cuts after a single line, which is most photo posts and shared posts. With 3 lines picked they still showed one line and a "more". Comments Facebook cuts that short get your count too. (#506)
* A shared post now stops at the lines you picked. Its text sits in a narrower box than the feed's, so with 3 lines picked it ran on to a 4th before the "more". (#506)
* Reel captions have a list of their own now, `Fold reel captions` under Reels and Watch, so the count you pick in `Fold long posts` stays on posts. Since 0.24.0 a reel's caption took the posts' count. The new list starts at Facebook's choice. (#506)
* `Show Comment when comments are off` works on Facebook 588 again. The Comment button down the side of a reel stayed hidden on reels whose author limited who can comment, even with the switch on. (#299)
* `Hide Threads cards between reels` also takes out the Meta promos Facebook now puts between reels, which is how the Threads card started showing up again for some people. (#85)
* `Next reel in the background` keeps going now. It used to play the reels Reels had loaded when you left and then the last one over and over, because Facebook stopped loading reels in the background. Reels loads more as it goes now, so new reels keep coming until you open Facebook again. (Discussion #524)
* `Next reel in the background` also moves on in Reels layouts where a reel reaches under the tab bar. There it played the same reel again. (Discussion #524)
* A reel's name, caption and buttons sit above the tab bar again after `Rounded tab bar` or `Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab` has been on. Turning either one off left every reel running a tab bar's height too tall, so the bottom of it hid behind the bar. (#551)
* The eye on stories stays on seen after you tap it. A second tap used to take the mark back, which was easy to do by accident, so now the eye dims after one tap and the mark goes with Facebook's next batch like before. (#557)
* `Post menu items` can take the `Skip silent parts` row out of a reel's More menu now. It joins the list once a reel's menu has shown it. A few reel rows that read like "Fb control type view edit privacy" on the list read plainly too. (#475)
* `Show reactions on the Like button` fits the box to the faces each post has. A post with one or two kinds of reactions got an empty gap before the faces, and its faces came out bigger than on a post with three. Now there's no gap and the faces are the same size everywhere. (#541)
* `Hide prompts and promos under reels` now cleans the row under a video in the feed too, where "Made with Edits" sat beside the poster's name. Remix, Use template, Stars and the other prompts it hides on reels go from that row as well, and `Hide the audio line on reels` takes the sound out of it. That row keeps a list of its own, so the switch never reached it before. (#314)
* With `Hide the audio line on reels` and `Show the post date on reels` both on, a reel keeps its date. The date sits on the audio line, so hiding the line took the date along with it. Now the line stays with just the date on it and the song left off. (#496)
* A long press on a reel's Download, Cast or Clean mode button no longer opens an empty gray box when your phone's font size is set above the default. With larger text Facebook shows a big copy of a button's label on a long press, and these buttons have no label under them, so the box came up empty. (#518)
* With `Only reels from accounts I follow` on, a video or reel you tap in the feed opens that video again, and so does a reel link someone sends you. When its author was someone you don't follow, the full screen player dropped it and showed another video instead, or stayed black with `Open feed videos on their own` on. The switch is meant for the Reels tab, so the video a player opens on stays now, and only the reels after it get filtered. (#565)
* The switches under `Tabs to hide` now point to `Menu tiles to hide`. Hiding a tab leaves its page in the Menu, which is the side panel on some accounts, and that list is what takes it out of there too. (#564)
* With `Hide the tab bar on the Reels tab` on, the first reels no longer stop short of the bottom with a black band under them. Facebook sizes them before the Reels tab opens, for the tab bar that's about to go. (#566)

## 0.24.0 (2026-11-22)

* This one moves to Facebook 588.0.0.42.55 and needs Morphe Manager 2.7.0 or newer, so update Manager and grab the new Facebook APK before you repatch. Every patch now goes in on the Android 9 and 10 build too. There's a lot new here. The next reel can play in the background, groups can keep the tab bar, and a tap on a feed video can bring up a pause button instead of opening Reels. Stories can lose their reply bar and Up next card, Facebook can go through a proxy you name (handy for VLESS), and a tablet can keep the feed in a column down the middle. On the fix side, reels fit the screen on a tablet held sideways and behind see-through bars, and `Play saved videos in a row` no longer stops on a saved post or shakes between videos. The tab bar also comes all the way back when you scroll to the top, and on Android 17 a close for using too much memory leaves a crash file and counts toward safe mode.

### Added

* `Next reel in the background` under Video and audio, for use with `Keep playing in the background`. When a reel playing in the background ends, the next reel Reels had loaded plays instead of the same one again, and you come back to Facebook on the last one played. Facebook doesn't load more reels while it's in the background, so once those run out the last one plays again. It starts off. (#524)
* `Keep the tab bar in groups` in the tab bar settings. Facebook opens a group on a page of its own with no tab bar, so the only way to another tab was Back. With this on, a group keeps the tab bar along the bottom, and tapping a tab takes you straight there. It works while the tab bar is at the bottom and starts off. (#503)
* `Show reactions on the Like button` under News feed moves a post's reaction icons from the far end of the row under it onto the Like button, right before the count, the way the summary line used to read. The button still likes and reacts like before. A video that shows its views there gets one Like icon, or your own reaction once you react. It starts off, and `Hide the reaction preview on posts` wins when both are on. (#541)
* `Tap shows a pause button on feed videos` under Video and audio stops a tap from opening a feed video in Reels. The tap brings up a big play/pause button in the middle of the video and the video keeps playing. Tap the button to pause or play, or tap the video again while it shows to open it. It starts off, and with Tap to play on it needs Inline feed video controls on too. (#538)
* `Hide the live video comment bar` under Comments and writing takes the comment box and the row of reactions off the bottom of a live video, so a stray tap won't send a reaction or open the keyboard. The live comments stay. It starts off. (#537)
* `Ignore taps beside the buttons` under News feed keeps a tap on the empty part of the row under a post, between the buttons and the reaction icons, from opening the comments or who reacted. Handy when you're aiming for a video's seek bar just above. It starts off. (#540)
* `Hide the Follow button` under Stories takes Follow off the top of a story from someone you don't follow. It starts off. (#543)
* `Hide the reply bar` under Stories takes the bar off the bottom of other people's stories, so the repost button, the Send message box and the quick reactions can't be tapped by accident. It starts off. (#542)
* `Hide the Up next card` under Stories starts the next stories right away, without the Up next card counting down over the first one. It starts off. (#544)
* New `Drag along the bottom edge` under Video and audio, for `Thin line seek bar`. While you drag the thin line it stays on the video's bottom edge, a bit thicker, with the time in the middle above it, instead of rising into the full bar. It starts off. (#539)
* `Hide posts with words you choose` and `Hide link posts` each have a switch for every place they reach (Home, the Reels tab, groups, profiles and Pages, and now search results). The first four start on, so nothing changes until you turn one off, and then that place keeps its posts while the others still lose theirs. Search results start off, since you usually want to see what you searched for. Turn it on and the posts on the Posts tab and in All go by your words or links too. (#458)
* `Ask before reacting to a story` under Stories makes a tap on a reaction at the bottom of a story ask "React to this story?" first. The story pauses while the question is up, and only Continue sends the reaction. Cancel, Back or a tap outside sends nothing and the story plays on. It's part of `Ask before a like` and starts off. (#502)
* `Hide friends' reactions on photos and videos` under News feed takes away the little picture of a friend with their reaction on it that Facebook puts in the corner of a photo or video someone you know reacted to. It's off until you turn it on. (#483)
* `Hide who reacted under posts` under News feed takes away the line under a post that names a friend or Page who reacted, like "Name and others reacted". It's off until you turn it on. (#483)
* `In-app proxy` under Privacy sends Facebook's connections through an HTTP proxy you name, like `127.0.0.1:10808`. A VLESS or V2Ray app's local port works there (v2rayNG's is `127.0.0.1:10808`), so Facebook can go through VLESS without the whole phone on a VPN. Leave it blank and Facebook connects the way your phone does. [Help](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/blob/main/docs/help.md#facebook-through-vless-or-another-proxy) walks through setting it up. (#504)
* `Ask before downloading` under Downloads puts a Continue or Cancel question in front of a reel's Download button, the Download button under a post and a video's `Download to phone`. It starts off. (#511)
* `Emoji font file` under Appearance lets you pick your own .ttf or .otf emoji font for `Use the phone's emoji`. Facebook needs a restart to pick it up, and a font with no emoji in it gets refused. (#513)
* A video opened from a post with several photos or videos now has `Download to phone` in the three-dot menu at the top. (#509)
* `Back to Clean mode` under Reels and Watch puts a reel back in Clean mode by itself after you bring its buttons back. Pick when: once the comments or a menu you opened close, or after 3, 5 or 10 seconds without touching the screen. It starts on Never. (#519)
* `Hide the line along the tab bar` under Tabs and navigation takes away the thin line Facebook draws along the edge of the tab bar, on every tab. It starts off, and a change shows on the next tab you tap. (#521)
* `Plain slide between people` under Stories turns the cube Facebook plays when you swipe from one person's stories to the next into a flat slide. Facebook only uses the cube on some accounts, and others already get a plain slide or a tilt. It's part of `Clean up the story viewer` and starts off. (#521)
* `Header text font` under Appearance picks the font your `Home header text` words draw in. There's Bold (how they've always looked), Regular, Serif, Script and Monospace, and `Your font` uses the font file you picked under `Font file`, even with `Use the system font` off. Facebook draws the header once, so a change shows after a restart. (#532)
* `Hide New for you on reels` under Reels and Watch takes "New for you", and the other reasons Facebook gives for showing you a reel, off the line under its author. It's part of `Clean up Reels` and starts off. With the label kept and `Show the post date on reels` on, the date now shows first, so you don't wait for the line to turn over to see when the reel was posted. (#520)
* `Translate every comment` under Comments and writing shows Facebook's translation on comments in another language as they load, the same as tapping See translation on each one. It comes with `Turn off auto-translation` and starts off. (#522)
* `Long press to save Marketplace photos` under Downloads saves a Marketplace listing's photo when you hold your finger on it, in the listing or opened full screen. It lands in the same folder as every other photo you save. It needs `Save any photo` and starts off. (#515)
* With `See-through bars` on, the strip behind the clock and battery icons is frosted glass too on Android 12 and newer, so the glass runs from the top bar up to the edge of the screen instead of stopping under a solid band. (#507)
* `Dim to` under Reels and Watch picks how much of a reel's buttons, caption and bars still shows once `Burn-in guard` dims them, from 0 to 100 percent. Left blank it stays at the 25 it always used. `Smooth fade` next to it eases them out and back in instead of switching at once, and it starts off. (#523)
* A crash deep in Facebook's native code or a freeze now leaves a `crash-` file in Download/Hushfacebook too, and so does Android 17 closing Facebook for using too much memory. Facebook writes it the next time it starts, even when that start runs paused, with what closed it and Android's own note about it. A freeze keeps the main thread's part of Android's trace. One from before your last update or reinstall says when that was and marks the versions unknown, since they could be the old build's. Before, only a Java crash got a file. (#528)
* `Video buffer` under Video and audio picks how much of a video loads before it starts and while it plays. Low starts a video once half a second has loaded and keeps less loaded ahead, so it uses less data. Medium waits for 1.5 seconds and Maximum for 3, and both keep more loaded ahead (up to a minute on Maximum), which can help when the connection is slow. It starts at Facebook's choice, which leaves everything as Facebook has it, and a change shows on the video that's playing within a moment. (#526)
* `Recently watched in the Menu` under Menu adds a Recently watched row to Settings and privacy in Facebook's Menu, right after Watch history. Tap it and the list `Remember what you watch` keeps opens over the Menu, so you don't have to go through the settings to get back to a reel or video. It needs `Resume long videos` and starts off. (#529)
* `Center the feed on big screens` under Appearance keeps the feed and Facebook's other lists in a column down the middle on a tablet or an unfolded phone, so posts stop stretching from one edge of the screen to the other. Reels still get the whole screen. It starts off. (#531)
* Four new switches for the top of a post, all off until you turn them on. `Show full names` under News feed lets a long name wrap onto a second line instead of ending in "...". `Hide songs under names` leaves the song out of the line under the name, with no gap where it was, and `Bold post times` makes the time or date there bold like the name. `Dark box behind feed video names` under Reels and Watch puts a name drawn on top of a feed video on the same dark box reel names can get. (#533)

### Changed

* Hushfacebook now patches Facebook 588.0.0.42.55 and no longer takes 587. On a 64-bit phone download the (arm64-v8a) (120-480dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 707615136, and on a 32-bit phone the (arm-v7a) (320dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 707615135. Update Facebook before you patch.
* You need Morphe Manager 2.7.0 or newer now, since the patches are built on Morphe patcher 2.5.0 and older Managers turn the bundle down. Update Manager first. Desktop patching needs morphe-desktop 2.3.0.
* **Tooling:** The GitHub release notes leave out the Tooling bullets and the What was checked paragraph. How to update opens with the Facebook build the release targets, which is the sentence the in-app update check reads.
* The README is about half as long. The patch list moved to [docs/patches.md](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/blob/main/docs/patches.md) and the help topics to [docs/help.md](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/blob/main/docs/help.md), and the README links to each topic.
* The changelog starts at 0.20.0 now, so Morphe Manager has a lot less to download when it shows what changed. Versions 0.1.0 to 0.19.0 are in the [changelog archive](https://github.com/SysAdminDoc/Hushfacebook/blob/main/docs/changelog-archive.md), word for word.
* `Hide create story cards` under Stories now also takes out the full screen "continue the story" card, the one asking you to share a recent moment from your gallery with a Create story button. (#521)
* With `Clean mode button on reels` on, Facebook's button for leaving Clean mode now sits right where the sidebar's Clean mode button was, so the same spot turns it on and off. It takes that spot from your last tap on the sidebar button in that viewer. (#391)
* `Hold a feed photo to react` and `Hold a feed video to react` open the reactions right by your finger now, instead of down by the Like button, so there's no long slide to reach one. Once you let go, a tap anywhere off them closes them without reacting. Their `Reaction` list has a new `Slide to pick one` too: slide to a reaction and let go, and letting go anywhere else closes them. (#454)
* The bug form now requires the Facebook file you patched. (#528)
* When a Debug report says why Facebook went away last time, it now names Android 17's two new reasons, the memory limiter and an anomaly, instead of showing a bare number. (#528)
* With `Keep playing in the background` on, a reel you swiped to in Reels keeps its sound after you leave Facebook. Before, only a video you'd started with a tap carried on, so a reel you'd swiped to went quiet. Reels playing in the feed or in a story still stop when you leave. (#524)
* **Tooling:** The build moves to Gradle 9.9.2, still pinned by checksum. 9.8.2 sits inside seven Gradle advisories from November, the worst being GHSA-3cfg-jpcr-gfp3, where a version catalog's values could run as code.
* **Tooling:** Guava moves to 33.8.0 and Bouncy Castle to 1.87, and the extension tests run on Robolectric 4.18.1. Guava 33.8.0 fixes GHSA-v5x9-67m3-7m56. Bouncy Castle 1.87 fixes the twelve CVEs its release notes list. Neither one ends up in the patches you install, since both only run while the bundle is built and tested. The release check also lists a Guava inside GHSA-v5x9 now, since that advisory isn't in the public database it asks.
* **Tooling:** gson moves to 2.15.0. It only runs while the patch list is written and the bundle is checked, so nothing you install changes. The patch tests stay on apksig 9.1.1, the version the patcher itself still brings.
* **Tooling:** The patches move off Morphe patcher's `bytecodePatch` and `resourcePatch` builders, which 2.5.0 marks deprecated and a later patcher will drop, to its `patch()`. Patch names, descriptions, defaults and options stay the same, so Manager keeps the patches you picked.
* **Tooling:** The register check that runs before a release now follows an object's class where two code paths meet, the way Android's own verifier does. A hook that hands a View parameter what Android sees as a plain Object now fails on the desktop instead of a phone rejecting the whole class at launch, which is how the cold-start tab bar fix first broke on a phone.

### Fixed

* With the tab bar at the bottom sliding away as you scroll, the tabs come all the way back when you scroll back to the top. After a short scroll near the top they used to stop short and sit over the gesture bar until you scrolled again. With `Tab bar while scrolling` on `Hides while scrolling`, Home also opens at the top after a restart. Facebook moved the feed down a little a few seconds after it opened, which slid both bars away before you'd touched anything.
* On a tablet or an unfolded phone held sideways, the reels you swipe to on the Reels tab fit the screen, with the caption, buttons and seek bar showing. Facebook works the reel's height out for the screen held upright, so turned on its side the bottom of every reel ran off the screen. (#531)
* `Play saved videos in a row` keeps going past a saved post. When the next card in Saved was a post and not a video, the post opened and the run of videos stopped there. Now that post closes again on its own and the card after it plays, and the screen stays black while it does, so you don't see the post flash by. Posts you open yourself stay open. (#404)

* Long-press a comment with a video in it and its menu has `Save video` now, when `Download any video` is on. It saves at the quality you picked under Downloads, like Download to phone on a post, and the file name leaves out who commented. With downloads set to send links instead, the row is `Send to app` (Refs #318).
* New `Fix washed-out video colors` under Video and audio, part of `Turn off HDR brightness`. On some phones a reel looks pale and faded until something like the comments covers part of it, and Android's `Disable HW overlays` developer option fixes it. This switch does the same for Facebook alone: videos draw together with the rest of the screen, which costs a little more battery. It's off by default and shows from the next video (Refs #308).
* New `Hide the search button` under Tabs and navigation takes the search button out of Home's top bar, and the buttons beside it close up. It works on both versions of the top bar Facebook hands out. It's off by default and shows after a restart (Refs #321).

Facebook only shows that list from the share count for some accounts, so this gets you there on any post that's been shared. It starts off, under News feed (Refs #283).

* New `Keep the name and buttons while seeking` leaves a reel's name, caption and side buttons on screen while you press its progress bar (#120). Off, Facebook clears them until you let go. It starts off, under Video and audio.

### Changed

* You need Morphe Manager 2.3.0 or newer now. The patches are built on Morphe patcher 2.1.0, the one Manager 2.3.0 ships with, and older Managers refuse a bundle built on it, so update Manager first. Desktop patching needs morphe-desktop 1.20.0 or newer.
* A build without `AMOLED black theme` says so under Appearance, in Colors and theme, so dark mode staying gray isn't a mystery. The row explains that Morphe Manager doesn't pick the theme by default and how to add it with Expert mode. The README also warns that **Enable recommended patches** unticks the theme, so it needs picking again after (Refs #275).
* Hushfacebook now patches Facebook 585.0.0.47.54 and no longer takes 584. On a 64-bit phone download the (arm64-v8a) (120-640dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 476015655, and on a 32-bit phone the (arm-v7a) (320dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 476015543. Update Facebook before you patch (Refs #309).
* The play/pause and cast buttons on feed videos are smaller now and look like Facebook's own speaker icon, a white symbol on a see-through round background instead of a big dark square. They sit closer to the video's corners and still take a tap as easily (Refs #301).
* The Hushfacebook settings take their colors and spacing from Astryx, Meta's own design kit. Cards are a soft neutral gray on the black page, the text and blue match Facebook's dark mode, and dialogs and buttons have rounder corners. Text on the blue (switches, the main dialog button) is dark now so it stays easy to read. The layout's the same, and the Material You theme still follows your wallpaper (Refs #302).
* With `Debug logging` on, a kept post that wasn't posted to a profile or group now says what it's made of (the kinds of things in its title, share and attachments, never names or text). That's what's needed to find group posts `Hide posts from groups you're not in` still lets through (Refs #195).
* The README's Help has a part on Google's developer verification, which since September 30 can stop a patched Facebook from installing on phones in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand. It covers Android's advanced flow and installing with adb from a computer, using the APK Manager exports, and asks for the exact message if Android refuses it some other way.
* **Tooling:** CONTRIBUTING has a Translating part now. It walks through fixing a string or adding a language: the table format, keeping placeholders, regenerating the Java, the catalog test to run and what to do with a string the code drops. It also covers checking a screen for clipped text with the two pseudo-locales. The README's language line points at it.
* **Tooling:** A few patches look harder at Facebook's code before they change it, and `Open on a chosen tab` names the methods it found when it stops. The device walk says which windows were in front when Facebook loses focus. It holds a reel with `Long press opens comments` and `Only on the right edge` off, so the hold really reaches 2x, and leaves the Comment button under posts so it can still open the comment sheet.
* **Tooling:** The register check now also looks at the objects each changed method hands on. When an injected call, field read or field write gets an object whose class the other side can't take, the check fails before the build gets near a phone. An early 585 build crashed at launch over exactly that, and dex2oat's verify pass didn't say a word about it.

* `Hide For you in search` and `Hide "People you may know" in search` now also reach the rows Facebook draws on the first page of the search screen before you type. On some accounts Facebook builds that first page on its own, apart from the rows further down, and the switches only ever saw the later ones, so For you stayed. Debug logging now says which part of Facebook handed over each row (Refs #285).

Facebook counted the frames a skip jumps over as frames its video decoder couldn't keep up with, and switched to the SD file. Those checks now sit out while silent parts are skipped, and a real decoder error still falls back the way it did (Refs #286).
* `Turn off double tap to like` now covers photos on Home too, so a double tap on a single photo, a collage or the photo flyout no longer likes the post or plays the heart (Refs #220).

* With `Always use Clean mode` on, the Clean mode button over a reel works both ways now. Before, its first tap brought the buttons back but the icon kept its "Turn off" label, and tapping it again did nothing. Now each tap switches Clean mode off or back on for the reel you're watching, and the label follows. Ads still don't get the button, same as in Facebook (Refs #304).
* `Hide prompts and promos under reels` now also takes away the "Made with Edits" line under the caption of a reel made in Meta's Edits app. Facebook draws that line on its own, apart from the chips under a reel, so the switch hid the Edits chip and missed the line (Refs #314).
* `Keep the original audio` now also works when Facebook gives you its Languages menu in the video player. * A reel's three-dot menu keeps its Quality row, with `Default playback quality` on or off. Facebook's player sometimes swaps its list of qualities for an empty one for a moment, and the menu only shows the row while that list has something in it, so a few seconds into some reels the row was gone until you scrolled away and back. Only the link goes over, never anything else Facebook attached to it, and only to the real app's own link screen. Part of `Restore screens on re-signed builds` (Refs #287).
