# Changelog

Changes in each Hushfacebook version, newest first.

## Unreleased

### Added

* **Facebook:** New `Speed on finished live videos` under Reels and Watch, part of `Keep the reel speed`. A live video that had ended opened in Facebook's live player with no Playback speed in its gear menu, so a long replay could only play at normal speed. Now the gear menu offers it like it does for other videos, with Facebook's own speed list behind it. Videos that are still live and ads keep Facebook's menu. It's on by default. (#238)
* **Facebook:** New `Back leaves Home where it is` under News feed. Pressing Back on a scrolled Home made Facebook jump to the top and reload, so the post you'd just commented on was gone. With the switch on, the feed stays put and a short message says to press Back again. A second Back within two seconds goes to the top the way Facebook always did. It's off by default and doesn't need `Keep feed position on return`. (#247)
* **Facebook:** New `Cast button on reels` under Reels and Watch. It puts a cast button on a reel's sidebar, under Download, and a tap hands the reel to a casting app you have, like Web Video Caster, the same way `Cast button on feed videos` does. It works with reel downloads off and starts off. (#182)
* **Facebook:** New `Local profile counts` under News feed. Type the friends, followers and following numbers you want and your own profile shows them on this phone, for fun. Nothing is sent anywhere, other people's profiles keep Facebook's real numbers, and leaving a row blank shows the real one. It starts off, and it changes numbers only when they sit next to the word Facebook shows for them, so a profile laid out another way may not change. A verified badge isn't part of it yet. (#280)
* **Facebook:** New `Round stories tray` under Stories. It draws the stories row on Home as round pictures, like Instagram, at a width you pick in dp. It starts off, and switched off the row looks exactly as Facebook draws it. Facebook has no round tile of its own, so each tile is sized to that width, square instead of tall, and clipped to a circle, and the row shrinks to one circle high with no blank band under it. (#260)
* **Facebook:** New `Most viewed on profile Reels` under Reels and Watch, part of `Newest reels first`. The Reels tab on a profile or a Page lists the reels it has loaded with the most viewed first, using the same play count Facebook shows on each tile, and sorts again as you scroll and more load. Reels with no count stay after the rest, and off keeps Facebook's newest-first order. It starts off. (Refs #232)
* **Facebook:** New `Block button on comments` under Comments and writing, part of `Comment sheet options`. The thumbs-down under a comment becomes a Block button. Tap it and Facebook's own block screen opens for the person who wrote the comment, with its usual question before anything is blocked. Nothing is disliked. With `Hide dislike on comments` on too, the Block button stays. It's off by default, and a change shows on comments drawn after it. (Refs #271)
* **Facebook:** New `Hide Like on comments` under Comments and writing, part of `Comment sheet options`. It takes the Like button out of the row under each comment. Reply stays, and so do the reaction icons and their count, so you can still see how people reacted. It starts off. (Refs #241)
* **Facebook:** New `Search date range` under Search. With it on, the Date posted filter in post search asks for a from and to month instead of a list of years, so you can narrow results to a stretch of time. Off, search is as Facebook has it. (Refs #279)
* **Facebook:** New `Burn-in guard` under Reels and Watch, part of `Clean up Reels`. While a reel or video fills the screen, its buttons, caption and bars fade when you leave them alone, so they don't burn into an OLED screen, and `Dim when idle and shift` also moves the screen a step every 2 minutes. The camera and the story editor are left alone. Tapping the video or double-tapping it to like doesn't bring them back. Tapping a button or swiping does. `Dim after` sets how long it waits, from at once to 10 seconds. With `Stay dim when I tap` on, they stay faded whatever you tap until you leave the video. It's off by default and needs no restart (Refs #288).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide the top right buttons on opened videos` under Reels and Watch, part of `Clean up Reels`. When you open a reel or a video full screen, including a video you tap open from Home, Search and the button next to it leave the top bar. The Reels tab's own Search and profile buttons stay. Back stays, and so does More at the bottom of the side buttons, where Download is. It's off by default and needs no restart (Refs #311).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide the pull down to lock hint` under Reels and Watch. Holding a reel or a video for 2x then shows the plain 2x pill without the "Pull down to lock 2x speed" line, and pulling down still locks the speed. It needs no restart (Refs #289).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide the emoji row on comments` under Comments and writing takes away the row of emoji Facebook puts over the comment box. Typing and the box's buttons stay, and you can still type emoji from your keyboard (Refs #290).
* **Facebook:** `Badge and logo colors` has a third box, `Selected tab color`, under Appearance. Type a color code like #2ECC71 and the selected tab in the tab bar takes it after a restart. It wins over Material You's accent when both are on, blank keeps Facebook's own color, and settings backups carry it (Refs #297).
* **Facebook:** New `Show Comment when comments are off` under Reels and Watch, part of `Clean up Reels`. Facebook takes the Comment button off a reel's sidebar when you can't comment on it, so the comments already there can't be read either. With the switch on the button stays, and a change shows from the next reel. It's off by default (Refs #299).
* **Facebook:** New `Show the post date on reels` under Reels and Watch, part of `Clean up Reels`. A reel, or a video you open full screen, says when it was posted, right under the name of whoever posted it. Facebook has that line built in but only turns it on for some accounts. It's off by default and shows from the next reel (Refs #296).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide the Subscribe button on videos` under Reels and Watch, part of `Clean up Reels`. Some authors get a Subscribe button next to their name on a full-screen video where others get Follow, and one tap subscribes you. With the switch on that button is gone, Follow buttons stay, and it needs no restart. It's off by default (Refs #291).
* **Facebook:** New `Select part of a post's text` under News feed. Holding a post's text still copies all of it, and with the switch on the text also opens in a box where you can select a word or a line and copy just that. It's off by default and needs no restart (Refs #294).
* **Facebook:** `Comment order` has a new choice, Oldest, under Comments and writing. Comments then open first comment first on every post, even one whose sort menu has no such row. Facebook's own menu calls that order All comments, so that's the row it shows picked (Refs #295).
* **Facebook:** New `Search with a picture` under Tabs and navigation. Press and hold the search button in the top bar and Google Lens opens, so you can point the camera at something or pick a picture to find out what it is. It uses the Google app when you have it and Lens in your browser when you don't. A tap still opens Facebook's search. The picture goes to Google, not Facebook. It's off by default and needs a restart (Refs #245).
* **Facebook:** The top of News feed in Hushfacebook's settings now shows how much the switches kept out of Home since Facebook started, with the three biggest kinds, like "41 posts and cards: ads (23), suggestions (12) and posts from groups you're not in (6)". It starts from zero each time Facebook does, nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and while Hushfacebook is paused the row says that instead.
* **Facebook:** New `Feed types to hide` under News feed, for people who read Debug logging. It lists the kinds of post and card Home showed since Facebook started that no switch hid, by Facebook's own names (like `FriendsLocationsFeedUnit`), and hides whichever ones you tick. A new kind of card Facebook adds can go before a switch exists for it. Ordinary posts aren't on the list, the post a link opened still stays, and settings files carry your picks. The row shows up while Debug logging is on, or once something's picked, and warns that picking a type Home needs can leave it empty.
* **Facebook:** New `Opening pause` at the top of Opening Facebook. When you open Facebook from its icon, a black screen asks "Still want to open Facebook?" and counts down 5 or 10 seconds (your pick in `Pause length`) before `Open now` works. `Close` or Back sends Facebook away, and opening it again starts the count over. Links, notifications and shares open Facebook straight away, and so does coming back within a minute or after the screen was off. If Lock Facebook is on too, the pause comes after you unlock. It's off by default, and settings backups carry the length.
* **Facebook:** `Recently watched` forgets old entries now. A new `Keep watched entries for` row under it picks 7 days, 30 days or until you clear the list, and new installs start at 30 days. A list you already had stays until you clear it, so nothing you kept disappears after the update. Each row on the list has a remove button, with Undo underneath to put it back where it was (unless you watched it again in the meantime). Entries age even with `Remember what you watch` off. Settings backups carry the time but never the entries.
* **Facebook:** New `Take breaks` under Opening Facebook. Once you've scrolled the feeds or Reels for the time you pick in `Break after` (10 to 60 minutes), a "Time for a break" screen covers them and counts down your `Break length`, 1 to 30 minutes. Chats, your profile and the Menu still work during a break, and the screen has buttons for the Menu, closing Facebook and the break settings. Time with the screen off doesn't count, being away as long as a break starts the count over, and a break keeps going if Facebook or the phone restarts. Turning the switch off or pausing Hushfacebook ends it. It's off by default, and settings backups carry both times (Refs #248).
* **Facebook:** Marketplace search reads rules now. Put a word in quotes, like `"word"`, to keep only listings with it in the title, put a minus in front, like `-word`, to hide listings with it, and add `in:place` (or `in:"two words"`) to keep listings in that place. A term matches anywhere in a title, so `-cat` hides cats too, and `"cat "` with a space after it leaves cats out. Facebook is sent only the plain words. An `Adv. Filters` button under Marketplace's own Filters opens three lists, words to keep, words to hide and places, each added as a tile you can take out again, and Go writes them into the search box and searches again. Both start off. `Advanced Marketplace search` under Marketplace turns the rules on, and `Adv. Filters button` adds the button once the rules are on. They come with `Hide sponsored Marketplace listings`. Contributed by Psychoninja1623.
* **Facebook:** New `Hide stories from Pages` under Stories keeps the Pages you follow out of the Stories tray, so it shows only people's stories. A Page's story goes whether Facebook suggests it or not, and so does one from a Page that's set up like a profile (National Geographic is one). It's off by default and takes effect the next time Facebook loads the tray (Refs #313).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide seen stories` under Stories takes the stories you've already watched all the way through out of the Stories tray, Pages' stories included. Your Add to story tile stays put. It's off by default too. `Keep seen posts hidden for` also has a longer choice now, 90 days, and the list of seen posts holds up to 20,000 so a busy feed doesn't push older ones out early (Refs #317).
* **Facebook:** `Hide suggested stories` and the two story switches above now reach the Stories tray at the top of Home too, and a single story Facebook drops into the feed. Before, they only cleaned up the other trays, so a Page's story could still show on Home (Refs #313, #317).
* **Facebook:** When those story switches leave Home's Stories tray with only a story or two, the tray now loads its next batch of stories on its own instead of sitting on three gray placeholder tiles. Facebook fetches more when one of the tray's last stories comes into view, but a row cut down to one story shows it before Facebook knows there's more, and on some accounts the gray tiles never ask. While the switches are hiding stories, a gray tile coming into view now loads the next batch too. If ten batches in a row bring nothing the switches keep, it stops asking (Refs #317).
* **Facebook:** New `Tap a reel above its comments` under Comments and writing. When a reel's comments open part way up, a tap on the reel above them plays or pauses it and the comments stay open. Before, that tap closed them. It works on the Reels tab and on reels you open from the feed, where the comments come up over the reel and the reel shows through above them. A drag there and the sound button still do what they did. With `Open comments at full height` on there's no reel left above the comments, so this one only matters with that switch off. It's off by default (Refs #315).
* **Facebook:** Long-press a comment with a video in it and its menu has `Save video` now, when `Download any video` is on. It saves at the quality you picked under Downloads, like Download to phone on a post, and the file name leaves out who commented. With downloads set to send links instead, the row is `Send to app` (Refs #318).
* **Facebook:** New `Fix washed-out video colors` under Video and audio, part of `Turn off HDR brightness`. On some phones a reel looks pale and faded until something like the comments covers part of it, and Android's `Disable HW overlays` developer option fixes it. This switch does the same for Facebook alone: videos draw together with the rest of the screen, which costs a little more battery. It's off by default and shows from the next video (Refs #308).
* **Facebook:** New `Hide the search button` under Tabs and navigation takes the search button out of Home's top bar, and the buttons beside it close up. It works on both versions of the top bar Facebook hands out. It's off by default and shows after a restart (Refs #321).
* **Facebook:** A Marketplace listing's page now says when it went up, after its place, like "Moncton, NB · Listed 3 weeks ago (Oct 12, 2026)". Facebook only says that on brand new listings, but it sends the time for every one. It's on by default as `Show when listings went up` under Marketplace and shows from the next listing you open (Refs #303).
* **Facebook:** New `See who shared` adds a row to a post's three-dot menu that opens Facebook's own list of the people who shared it, for photo posts and videos. Facebook only shows that list from the share count for some accounts, so this gets you there on any post that's been shared. It starts off, under News feed (Refs #283).
* **Facebook:** Two new story switches under Video and audio. `Mute stories` (with `Keep feed videos muted`) starts every story with the sound off each time you open Facebook, until you tap a speaker or press volume up. `Video stories wait for a tap` (with `Tap to play`) keeps a video story from playing by itself, so it won't start until you touch and hold it and let go. Both start off and work with the switches above them on or off (Refs #261).

### Changed

* **Facebook:** You need Morphe Manager 2.3.0 or newer now. The patches are built on Morphe patcher 2.1.0, the one Manager 2.3.0 ships with, and older Managers refuse a bundle built on it, so update Manager first. Desktop patching needs morphe-desktop 1.20.0 or newer.
* **Facebook:** A build without `AMOLED black theme` says so under Appearance, in Colors and theme, so dark mode staying gray isn't a mystery. The row explains that Morphe Manager doesn't pick the theme by default and how to add it with Expert mode. The README also warns that **Enable recommended patches** unticks the theme, so it needs picking again after (Refs #275).
* **Facebook:** Hushfacebook now patches Facebook 585.0.0.47.54 and no longer takes 584. On a 64-bit phone download the (arm64-v8a) (120-640dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 476015655, and on a 32-bit phone the (arm-v7a) (320dpi) (Android 11+) bundle, build 476015543. Update Facebook before you patch (Refs #309).
* **Facebook:** The play/pause and cast buttons on feed videos are smaller now and look like Facebook's own speaker icon, a white symbol on a see-through round background instead of a big dark square. They sit closer to the video's corners and still take a tap as easily (Refs #301).
* **Facebook:** The Hushfacebook settings take their colors and spacing from Astryx, Meta's own design kit. Cards are a soft neutral gray on the black page, the text and blue match Facebook's dark mode, and dialogs and buttons have rounder corners. Text on the blue (switches, the main dialog button) is dark now so it stays easy to read. The layout's the same, and the Material You theme still follows your wallpaper (Refs #302).
* **Facebook:** With `Debug logging` on, a kept post that wasn't posted to a profile or group now says what it's made of (the kinds of things in its title, share and attachments, never names or text). That's what's needed to find group posts `Hide posts from groups you're not in` still lets through (Refs #195).
* **Facebook:** The README's Help has a part on Google's developer verification, which since September 30 can stop a patched Facebook from installing on phones in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand. It covers Android's advanced flow and installing with adb from a computer, using the APK Manager exports, and asks for the exact message if Android refuses it some other way.
* **Tooling:** CONTRIBUTING has a Translating part now. It walks through fixing a string or adding a language: the table format, keeping placeholders, regenerating the Java, the catalog test to run and what to do with a string the code drops. It also covers checking a screen for clipped text with the two pseudo-locales. The README's language line points at it.
* **Tooling:** A few patches look harder at Facebook's code before they change it, and `Open on a chosen tab` names the methods it found when it stops. The device walk says which windows were in front when Facebook loses focus. It holds a reel with `Long press opens comments` and `Only on the right edge` off, so the hold really reaches 2x, and leaves the Comment button under posts so it can still open the comment sheet.
* **Tooling:** The register check now also looks at the objects each changed method hands on. When an injected call, field read or field write gets an object whose class the other side can't take, the check fails before the build gets near a phone. An early 585 build crashed at launch over exactly that, and dex2oat's verify pass didn't say a word about it.

### Fixed
* **Facebook:** With `Tab bar at the top` on, the Reels tab now runs the reel under the bar instead of starting it a bar-height lower, so the video fills the screen like it does with the bar at the bottom. The other tabs and the bottom bar are unchanged. (#264)
* **Facebook:** With `Hide For you in search` and `Hide "People you may know" in search` on, the search screen no longer sits on "No recent searches" with a spinner that never stops, and pulling down no longer restarts it. When every row Facebook has loaded is one of the hidden ones, the screen now stops asking for more. Recent searches and any other row still load as before. (#235)

* **Facebook:** `Hide For you in search` and `Hide "People you may know" in search` now also reach the rows Facebook draws on the first page of the search screen before you type. On some accounts Facebook builds that first page on its own, apart from the rows further down, and the switches only ever saw the later ones, so For you stayed. Debug logging now says which part of Facebook handed over each row (Refs #285).
* **Facebook:** With `Skip silent parts` on, reels no longer drop to low quality a few seconds in and lose their Quality row. Facebook counted the frames a skip jumps over as frames its video decoder couldn't keep up with, and switched to the SD file. Those checks now sit out while silent parts are skipped, and a real decoder error still falls back the way it did (Refs #286).
* **Facebook:** `Turn off double tap to like` now covers photos on Home too, so a double tap on a single photo, a collage or the photo flyout no longer likes the post or plays the heart (Refs #220).
* **Facebook:** `Hide posts from groups you're not in` now also catches group posts that only name their group next to the poster's name or point to it in an attachment, and Debug logging shows which part of the post named the group (Refs #195).
* **Facebook:** With `Always use Clean mode` on, the Clean mode button over a reel works both ways now. Before, its first tap brought the buttons back but the icon kept its "Turn off" label, and tapping it again did nothing. Now each tap switches Clean mode off or back on for the reel you're watching, and the label follows. Ads still don't get the button, same as in Facebook (Refs #304).
* **Facebook:** `Hide prompts and promos under reels` now also takes away the "Made with Edits" line under the caption of a reel made in Meta's Edits app. Facebook draws that line on its own, apart from the chips under a reel, so the switch hid the Edits chip and missed the line (Refs #314).
* **Facebook:** `Keep the original audio` now also works when Facebook gives you its Languages menu in the video player. With that menu on, Facebook kept every dubbed track of a reel and played the one in your phone's language, so the switch changed nothing. Now the reel keeps only the track it was made with. With the switch off Facebook picks the audio the way it always has (Refs #148).
* **Facebook:** A reel's three-dot menu keeps its Quality row, with `Default playback quality` on or off. Facebook's player sometimes swaps its list of qualities for an empty one for a moment, and the menu only shows the row while that list has something in it, so a few seconds into some reels the row was gone until you scrolled away and back. An empty list now leaves the last full one in place (Refs #286).
* **Facebook:** With `View stories anonymously` on, a story you watched to the end is grey from the moment Facebook opens again. When it starts, Facebook draws the Stories tray from a quick first copy before it loads the real one, and that copy never got marked, so a watched ring showed blue for a while after each restart and went grey a few refreshes later (Refs #165).
* **Facebook:** A Page's Events link, like facebook.com/facebook/events, opens that Page on its Events tab now. Facebook's own server sends every one of those links to Home, so tapping one in a post or opening it from another app dropped you on the feed. Part of `Open links in external browser`, and it works whichever way its switches are set (Refs #319).
* **Facebook:** An Instagram or Threads link in a post or a comment opens a patched Instagram or Threads now. Facebook only hands those links to an app Meta signed, so with a repatched one the tap did nothing. Only the link goes over, never anything else Facebook attached to it, and only to the real app's own link screen. Part of `Restore screens on re-signed builds` (Refs #287).
* **Facebook:** The Messenger icon in the top bar opens a repatched Messenger installed in place of Meta's, like HushMessenger over `com.facebook.orca`, even with `Open the Messenger app` off. Facebook only hands that tap to a Messenger Meta signed, so with a repatched one the icon did nothing and the screen flickered. Meta's own Messenger and a Messenger installed beside it are handled as before (Refs #292).
* **Facebook:** `Hide comments under posts` now also hides a pinned comment shown under a post in the feed, along with the Write a comment box it comes with. Facebook draws that comment as part of the comment box, not with the other comment previews, so the switch missed it under friends' posts and group posts. The Comment button still opens the comments as before (Refs #305, #253).
* **Facebook:** With `See-through bars` on, the blur on the top and bottom bars no longer turns into a dark strip when a video in the feed starts or scrolls into view. The bars used to drop their blur whenever any video showed on the page, so it seemed to come and go at random as you scrolled. Now the blur stays and only the video itself is left out of it (Refs #250).
* **Facebook:** `See-through bars` works on a tab bar that's set to stay on screen now. Before, the bar stayed but the feed stopped above it, so the see-through showed black. Facebook builds that layout as it starts, so flipping `See-through bars` with the bar kept brings up the restart row, and the change shows after the restart (Refs #250).
* **Facebook:** `Hide posts from groups you're not in` now also hides a group post that reaches your feed because a friend commented on it or shared it. Those come wrapped in the friend's activity, so the switch couldn't see the group before (Refs #195).
* **Facebook:** `Hide posts from groups you're not in` also catches a group post that names its group in a second place. Some posts come with the group listed apart from where the post was made, and the switch only looked at the first one, so those stayed in the feed (Refs #195).
* **Facebook:** Reels get the Download button on accounts where Facebook still draws its older reel buttons. Facebook picks one of two button layouts for each account, and the older one never had a place for the button, so those reels showed only Facebook's own buttons. With the button on, reels now open with the newer layout and the button is there. Turn off `Download button on reels` and Facebook's layout comes back as it was (Refs #316, #298).
* **Facebook:** A patched Facebook without `Material You theme` no longer shows a toast about the theme's colors at every start on Android 11. The theme only asks the phone for its palette when it's in the build, and the Accent color presets are set up before the theme reads them (Refs #300).
* **Facebook:** Opening a link to a post from another app, a message or a notification shows that post at the top of Home again. Facebook sends it as the first post of the feed, flagged like a suggested post when you don't follow its author, so `Hide "Suggested for you" posts` hid the one post you asked for. The first post after a link lands now stays whatever switch would hide it, ads aside (Refs #306).
* **Facebook:** `Hide dislike on comments` leaves a comment's reaction icons and their count alone now. 0.19.0 took the thumbs-down out by telling Facebook the button wasn't there, and the reactions next to it went too. The button is built empty instead, so the rest of the row is drawn the way it is next to a dislike (Refs #241).
* **Facebook:** `Inline feed video controls` shows its play button with `Tap to play` on too. Tap to play made Facebook's own Autoplay setting read Off, and with that the feed never set up a player for its videos, so the button had nothing to start. With both on, Autoplay now stays the way you set it while videos still wait for your tap, and the button starts the video right where it sits. A video that hasn't drawn yet gets the button too (Refs #239).
* **Facebook:** Going back to the top of Home with an inline video button showing no longer crashes Facebook. The feed could drop the video's row in the middle of drawing the screen, and the button came off at that very moment, which Android's layout doesn't allow. It hides right away now and comes off once the screen is drawn (Refs #239).
* **Facebook:** With `Tap to play` on, a video you tap in the feed opens full screen playing on slower phones too. Facebook's full screen player only starts once it has loaded, and on some phones that took longer than the one second a tap gets, so the video sat paused at 0:00 until a second tap. The tap that opened the player now counts for its first video for up to ten seconds, and a swipe or a second video still waits (Refs #239).
* **Facebook:** With `Open feed videos on their own` on, a reel you open from a group you're not in plays right away now, with no black loading screen first. The switch skipped the step where Facebook starts loading that video, so the viewer sat black until the video came in some other way. That step goes through now, and the reels Facebook would line up after the video still stay out, with no "Up next" pill under it (Refs #237).
* **Facebook:** TalkBack reads a settings row that only shows something, like the hidden count at the top of News feed, as one stop now. It used to read the title and then the count as two separate stops. A save in progress on the Downloads page works the same way, with its Cancel button still a stop of its own. With a screen reader on, that row's amount and time now move every ten seconds instead of every second, because TalkBack read the row again at each change and never got a pause. A new step, like joining the picture and sound, still shows right away.
* **Facebook:** Debug logging for `Keep the progress bar` names a reel by a short code now, not by its Facebook video id. The lines still pair up when one reel hands the bar to the next, but a shared log doesn't show which reels you watched.
* **Tooling:** The file listing the supported Facebook builds and the patch list generator credit where they really came from, De-Vanced, by way of tiktok-patches-for-morphe and Hushfeed. Their old notices pointed at ReVanced files that never existed. NOTICE and the app's Licenses page name De-Vanced now, and the mirror check fails a cited file that GitLab sends to a folder page instead.
* **Tooling:** The build moves to Gradle 9.8.2, still pinned by checksum. It's a bug-fix release, and the wrapper jar is the same file as 9.8.1's.
* **Facebook:** `Turn off HDR brightness` turns PQ videos down on Snapdragon phones again, apart from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 1. All of them were left out after that chip's video service crashed when asked to tone-map, and a Snapdragon 8 Elite turns out to do the job fine. HLG videos are handled as they were. Checked in tests and with a decoder test on a Galaxy S25: PQ samples in HEVC, VP9 and AV1 all came out at the usual range, and its video service kept running.

## 0.19.0 (2026-10-27)

* **Facebook:** This one needs Morphe Manager 2.2.0 or newer, so update Manager before you patch. Posts can get Save and Download buttons under them, and new switches take Like, Comment, Share or Send off posts and reels, hide posts from Pages or drop the dislike on comments. Feed videos can get a cast button, downloads can go to an SD card, and 32-bit phones work now. You can also pick your own badge and logo colors, sort reels by most liked and copy a reel's whole caption.

### Added

* **Facebook:** New `Badge and logo colors` lets you pick your own color for the red count badges on the tabs and for the blue Facebook logo at the top of Home. Type a color code like #2ECC71 in `Badge color` or `Logo color` under Appearance and restart Facebook. Left blank, both keep Facebook's colors, and settings backups carry them (Refs #263).
* **Facebook:** Facebook 584.0.0.51.56 now works on 32-bit phones too. The armeabi-v7a Android 11+ bundle, build 475817404, is declared next to the arm64 build 475817393, and every patch applies to it. Contributed by toidicakhia (Refs #254).
* **Facebook:** `Hide the Create button` under Tabs and navigation takes the + button for new posts, reels and stories out of the top bar, on both versions of the bar Facebook hands out. It starts off and shows after a restart (Refs #257).
* **Facebook:** A reel's More menu has `Copy caption` now. It copies the whole caption, including the part hidden behind See more, so there's no more screenshotting a long caption to get its text. It's on by default, and `Copy reel captions` under Reels and Watch turns it off (Refs #240).
